About RuloSport

About

Protect | Play | Perform. RuloSport makes overgrips and frame protection designed specifically for padel.

We started on the courts in Melbourne, testing gear with players and coaches and asking a simple question: why are accessories so often an afterthought? Rulo is our answer. Thin, tacky grips that keep the feel of your racket, and edge tape that protects your frame without getting in the way.
