FAQ

Quick answers about our grips, edge tape and wholesale.

Overgrips

How thin are Rulo Pro Overgrips?
0.6mm. They add grip without noticeably building up the handle, so your racket keeps the feel you're used to.
How often should I change my overgrip?
It depends on how often you play. Change it when it starts to feel slippery, shiny or worn. For frequent players that's often every few weeks.
Will they fit my racket?
Yes. Overgrips wrap over your racket's existing handle, so they fit any padel racket.

Edge tape

What's the difference between Edge Lite and Edge Carbon?
Edge Lite is translucent TPU. It protects your frame while letting your racket's design show through. Edge Carbon is our premium option with a black 3K carbon finish.
Why use edge tape?
Padel rackets regularly take knocks from walls, glass and the floor. Edge tape adds a protective layer around the frame to help reduce chips and scuffs.
How do I apply it?
  1. Clean the racket surface.
  2. Peel off the backing of the protection tape.
  3. Centre the tape and apply from the middle first.
  4. Push the sides down onto the racket.
  5. Replace as needed.
