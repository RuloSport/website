How to regrip

  1. Remove your old overgrip and wipe the handle clean.
  2. Peel the backing and start at the butt cap with the tapered end.
  3. Wrap up the handle, overlapping each turn slightly and keeping light tension.
  4. Trim if needed and secure the end with the finishing tape.
